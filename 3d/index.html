About

Hi, I'm Adwittiya

I’ve been drawing since before I could read. Fine arts training since 2012 is what sits under every 3D render I make.

I'm a junior 3D artist and a CG Animation & VFX graduate of Collarts in Melbourne. I've trained in fine arts since 2012, so most of my work starts on paper before it reaches a viewport.

I work across the pipeline: scriptwriting, character design, modelling and texturing, lighting, FX and art direction. I've done it inside collaborative studio-style teams, from concept to final render. I also write Python and HTML, which helps on technical and pipeline work.

Right now I'm art director and lighting artist on a 2-minute advocacy animation for Animal-Free Science Advocacy.
