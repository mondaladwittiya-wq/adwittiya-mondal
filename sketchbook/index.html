about me

Hi, I'm Adwittiya

I’ve been drawing since before I could read. Fine arts training since 2012 is what sits under every character I design.

I'm a junior 2D character designer and storyboard artist, and a CG Animation & VFX graduate of Collarts in Melbourne.

I design characters from silhouette to turnaround, plan films as thumbnails, storyboards and colour scripts, and animate in 2D, from squash-and-stretch tests to an After Effects welcome video sent to more than 1,000 students.

Right now I'm art director and lighting artist on a 2-minute advocacy animation for Animal-Free Science Advocacy.
