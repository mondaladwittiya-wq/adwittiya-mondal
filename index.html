About me

Fine Arts training since 2012 CG Animation & VFX Graduate, Collarts, Melbourne Melbourne, Australia

I’m Adwittiya, a 2D animator and CG Animation & VFX graduate from the Australian College of the Arts (Collarts) in Melbourne. I’ve been drawing since 2012, trained in fine arts through Pracheen Kala Kendra, and that traditional foundation is what gives my animation its life.

I specialise in character animation, frame-by-frame work, storyboarding and animatics: the kind of work that starts on paper and earns its soul before it ever reaches a screen. I’ve contributed across full production pipelines, from scriptwriting and character design to timing, clean-up and compositing, both in collaborative studio teams and independently.

Most recently I’ve been animating and art directing a 2-minute advocacy film for Animal-Free Science Advocacy, and before that I wrote, designed and animated Collarts’ official student welcome video, seen by 1,000+ incoming students. I also design original 2D character and card assets for an independent video game.

I’m currently based in Melbourne and open to 2D animation roles, character animation work and collaborative productions.

What I Do

Four ways I can help your production, all from work I've actually shipped.

Character Animation

Personality-driven character animation with expressive timing, rooted in a traditional fine arts foundation. I've worked across 2D digital and frame-by-frame techniques.

Storyboarding & Animatics

From thumbnail sketches to fully timed animatics with sound. I plan sequences as real sets, with floor plans, camera positions and colour scripts, before a single frame is animated.

Frame-by-Frame / Traditional

Hand-drawn animation with warmth and texture you can feel. Fine arts trained since 2012, with a deep understanding of weight, life and line.

Scriptwriting & Production

Full pipeline contribution from script to screen. I've written narrative structure, dialogue and scene direction for short film and studio productions.
